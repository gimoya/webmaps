    Usage

    Events
    Pick an event before riding or viewing. The event id is the first part of the URL hash and will be remembered so you don't need to pick after each new visit.
    Start & stop riding
    after klicking the 'Biker Icon' the button 'Start/Resume' begins or resumes your trace. Stop ends the ride and leaves the trace on the map. Caution (!): That name/alias cannot start again! Caution (!): If you want a ranking on a loaded route, you need to stop your ride to fix your placement!
    Logging
    As long as you keep this page open and online and given, that you permitted GPS Sensor usage, you location will be logged! A refresh, close or a locked screen stops logging and you will be asked to resume when you come back to page.
    Name
    Use your own unique name or alias. Caution (!): A name or alias that seomone else has already in use, then you would mix into their trace and don't have your own!
    Points
    A point is stored about every 5 s if you moved at least 5 m. If you move slowly (less than 5 m in 5 s) or when you have the browser closed and dont write your locations, other viewers see 'Tracking paused'.
