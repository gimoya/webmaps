      How it works
      Rider locations are logged and displayed on the map as a trace. The rider's position will only be logged when they are looking at the map with there GPS turned on, and the browser is given rights to use the GPS signal (you will be asked to give permission to use the GPS sensor).
      Alias
      The name you enter is the identity used. You will find the alias on your trace in the map and in the list of tracked users in the panel. Always use your own unique alias - using an alias someone else is already tracking can corrupt their trace.
      Start/Resume/Stop
      The Rider box starts a new trace, or resumes one already active for that name. Stop tracking ends the trace and removes it from the list and map.
      No tracking
      A fresh webpage load, a browser refresh, closing the webpage or a screen look stops this device/browser from tracing your GPS.
      Stale signal
      A point is stored about every 5 seconds. If no new point is stored for more than 5 seconds, the trace stays on the map at the last known position and the list marks it Tracking paused.
      Routes
      Open Routes menu and pick one to draw that track on the map. You can load GPX files whic will be added to available routes.
