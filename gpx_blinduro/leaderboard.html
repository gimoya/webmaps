Each cell shows the rider's rank in each segment. The brackets show the bonus points for the first 3 places (-3, -2, -1) and the penalty points (+2) for segments not ridden (DNS: did not start), which will be added to the rank score. For the overall score, each segment's rank score is summed. The lowest total sum in the bottom row wins!

The different strategies for you:

Some riders may look to accomplish as many segments as possible without caring for best ranks. They will score middle but avoid DNS penalties.

Alternatively, one could focus on achieving top placements and good bonus points by winning only a few segments and skipping others.

Then there is this special strategy: look out for niche segments with few competitors — here chances for good ranks and bonus points are higher.. And you'll increase DNS penalties to other riders.
