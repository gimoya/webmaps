How does it work?
Upload your GPX file. The app matches your track against defined segments (start/end checkpoints). When your track passes through both within the tolerance distance (25 m), you get a segment match with duration and travelled distance. After matching, you can submit to the leaderboard.
How do the Start and End Areas work?
For calculating your segment's duration and travelled distance, the last track point before leaving the Start Area and the first track point after entering the End Area are used.
Do I need timestamps in my GPX?
Yes. Segment duration is computed from track point timestamps. GPX files without timestamps cannot be matched.
Multiple laps?
If you do the same segment repeatedly, each lap is matched separately. You get one result per lap, each submittable.
Why is GPX required for submission?
Your GPX is not only needed for matching of segments but also stored as proof for your ranking. It archives the ride that produced the result.
Can I download GPX from the leaderboard?
Yes. Top 10 entries per segment show a GPX button to download the proof file.
