Clean Google Maps & Earth KML/KMZ online

Exports from Google Maps, My Maps, or Google Earth are often multilayered and messy: folders, mixed paths and placemarks, leftovers you never meant to ship. track toolbox is built for that cleanup ↔ publish loop.

Open track toolbox

The two directions

  • In: Pull KML/KMZ from Maps or Earth → multi-select what matters → trim, split, rename, remove noise → export a clean set of tracks and points.
  • Out: Collect a tidy set here → export KML ready to import back into Google Maps / Earth, with tracks and waypoints kept separate when you need that discipline.

Typical workflow

  1. Export or download your KML/KMZ from Maps, My Maps, or Earth.
  2. In track toolbox, use Upload files (or u). KMZ/ZIP works.
  3. Select features on the map or in the list; use Shift+drag to add everything in a rectangle.
  4. Remove what you don’t need. Edit paths (trim / scissor / nodes) via right-click or e.
  5. Rename the keepers. Filter by name if the list is long.
  6. Export KML (or GPX). Choose separate files or a feature collection; waypoints go to a points file so tracks and points stay clean.

Who needs this

Tourism teams packaging rides for Maps, event managers shipping course lines and aid points, guides maintaining a library, planners fixing a decade of My Maps layers, athletes cleaning a shared Earth dump before race week.

FAQ

How do I clean a messy KML or KMZ from Google Maps?
Upload it, select what to keep, edit or delete the rest, export clean KML/GPX. Everything stays in your browser.

Can I re-import into Maps or Earth?
Yes — export KML from track toolbox and import that clean set.

Is this a Google account tool?
No. No login. You bring the file; you take the file.
