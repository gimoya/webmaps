GPX & KML job guides

track 📐toolbox is a free browser tool for fast multi-track and waypoint surgery: load, select, cut, rename, convert, digitize, export. These pages match the jobs people actually search for.

Open track toolbox

Guides

  • Clean Google Maps / Earth KML & KMZ — multilayered My Maps dumps in; clean tracks and points out (and back into Maps/Earth).
  • Trim & split GPX online — cut bad GPS, scissor tracks, delete nodes, export device-ready files.
  • GPX ↔ KML converter & editor — convert and edit in one flow; separate tracks vs waypoints when you export.
  • track toolbox vs gpx.studio — when to use the workshop vs the surgical toolbox.

Who this is for

Trail builders, tourism and destination teams, guiding venues, event managers, planners, serious riders and athletes — anyone who needs to multi-select, edit, convert, and export GPX/KML quickly without a full tour-planning app.

Privacy

Files are processed in your browser. No account required.
