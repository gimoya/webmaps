GPX & KML job guides

The lightest browser tool for cleaning and converting GPX and KML. Load, edit, cut, crop, reroute, export. No account, no ads, no install. Tips are highly appreciated and needed to keep the tool running.

The router is the headline: draw stations, BRouter builds the path, save a real track. It also works on a phone. These pages are the jobs people actually search for.

Guides

  • Draw a route with BRouter — click stations, get a path (MTB, trekking, hiking) in the Alps window, then edit and export it like any other track.
  • On your phone — the same toolbox with taps and long-press. No install.
  • Clean Google Maps / Earth KML & KMZ — multilayered My Maps dumps in; clean tracks and points out (and back into Maps/Earth).
  • Trim & split GPX online — cut bad GPS, scissor tracks, delete nodes, export device-ready files.
  • GPX ↔ KML converter & editor — convert and edit in one flow; import FIT activities; separate tracks vs waypoints when you export.
  • track toolbox vs gpx.studio — when to use the workshop vs the light cleaner/exporter.

Who this is for

Trail builders, tourism and destination teams, guiding venues, event managers, planners, serious riders and athletes — anyone who needs to multi-select, edit, convert, and export GPX/KML quickly without a full tour-planning app.

Privacy

Files are processed in your browser. No account required.
