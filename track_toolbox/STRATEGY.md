# track 📐toolbox — visibility & SEO strategy

Companion to [`PITCH.md`](PITCH.md). Goal: be **detectable at the moment of pain**, not “another GPX app” in a directory.

## Principle

Target users don’t browse GPX tools casually. They hit a concrete job and search or ask in a club:

- messy Google Maps / Earth KML/KMZ
- trim / split / clean a GPS dump
- GPX ↔ KML for devices or Maps
- multi-file select → export clean set

**Detection = search + trust channels.** Own those job phrases; show up where the audience already is.

Titles and H1s should state the **job**, not only the brand.

## Comparison positioning

Do **not** claim to replace gpx.studio. Capture “I need something faster for cleanup”:

- studio = workshop (elevation, time, merge, POI icons)
- track toolbox = surgical toolbox (multi-select, trim/split session, KML/KMZ, Google loop)

Page: [`seo/vs-gpx-studio.html`](seo/vs-gpx-studio.html).

## Trust for agencies & events

- Files stay in the browser (say it on every SEO page)
- Docs + “Who is this for?”
- Named use cases: event course, tourism package, Maps re-import
- Collect 3 real testimonials early (“Used by …”)

## What not to prioritize yet

- Broad Product Hunt / App Store as primary discovery
- Paid search until organic job pages convert
- Competing with Komoot/Strava on “plan a ride” SEO

## Highest ROI order

1. Keep `seo/` job pages indexed (sitemap + internal links from docs/app).
2. Link from every Tirol Trailhead GPX touchpoint.
3. 3–5 community posts with a before/after GIF (trail + event + Maps users).
4. Push the vs-gpx.studio page when people ask for alternatives.
5. Collect 3 testimonials (agency / event / guide).

## Success signals

- Organic landings on `seo/*` that click through to the app
- Referrals from Tirol Trailhead trail/event pages
- Inbound “we cleaned our My Maps dump with …” mentions
- Repeat use by clubs / events (not one-off curiosity traffic)
